      Measuring

      Whole grid
      –Grid order φ
      –Orientation entropy
      –Length within 2° of N·S·E·W
      0 · no order1 · perfect grid

      φ rescales entropy so that a perfect four-way grid scores 1 and streets pointing every which way score 0 (Boeing 2019). Entropy runs from 1.386 for a perfect grid to 3.584 for uniform bearings.

      Size and shape

      Segment lengths (log scale)

      One address block is 100 house numbers, or ⅛ mile (660 ft), on Chicago's 800-per-mile grid.

      Intersections

      Nodes come from segment endpoints snapped to 1 ft, as in chicago_streets_stats.py. A degree-2 node is where one segment hands off to the next. Area selections cut segments at the edge, which shows up as extra dead ends.

      Shortest streets

      Citywide, shortest first.

      Ordinary streets only: every segment a surface public street (class 2, 3, 4, 5 or 7, normal status), no ramps, turn lanes, access roads, busways or airport roads. Length is the distance between the street's two far ends, as in the project's shortest-street list. Click a row to see the street.

      Longest streets here

      Click a row to show that street on its own.

      Data. Street Center Lines from the Chicago Data Portal (56,336 segments, last updated by the city in 2016), served from this site as data/streets.geojson.
      Method. Same measures as chicago_streets_stats.py. Coordinates are projected to feet around State & Madison, the zero point of Chicago's address grid, so the readout shows approximate addresses. Bearings from latitude/longitude data use true north; shapefiles in Illinois State Plane use grid north, about 0.45° different.
